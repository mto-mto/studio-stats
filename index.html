Studio Stats

A personal, read-only tool that reads the YouTube Analytics of its owner's own channel.

What it does

Studio Stats is a small command-line program that the owner of a YouTube channel runs on their own computer. It reads the channel's analytics (views, watch time, thumbnail impressions, click-through rate and audience retention for each video) and writes a report the owner uses to plan their next videos.

Read-only

It asks Google only for read-only access (yt-analytics.readonly and youtube.readonly). It can't upload, edit, delete or post anything, and it doesn't read email, contacts or any other Google data.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the channel, on their own account. It isn't offered to the public and has no sign-up.

YouTube API Services

Studio Stats uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
